# Sunrise Dental Care — Patient Handbook

Sample document for demos and evaluation. Sunrise Dental Care is a fictional clinic.

## About the clinic

Sunrise Dental Care is a family dental practice with two branches in Hyderabad.
We have treated patients since 2011 and offer general, cosmetic and children's
dentistry under one roof. Our team speaks English, Hindi, Telugu and Urdu.

## Branches and opening hours

### Banjara Hills branch
Address: Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad 500034, opposite City Centre Mall.
Open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed on Sundays.
Free visitor parking is available in the building basement for up to two hours.

### Gachibowli branch
Address: 3rd floor, Silver Oak Plaza, Gachibowli, Hyderabad 500032.
Open Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
The Gachibowli branch is closed on Saturdays.
Paid parking is available at the plaza for 40 rupees per hour.

Both branches are closed on national holidays: Republic Day, Independence Day,
Gandhi Jayanti and Diwali.

## Services and prices

All prices are in Indian rupees and include GST.

- Consultation and check-up: 500 rupees. The consultation fee is waived if you
  start treatment on the same day.
- Teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing): 1,200 rupees.
- Tooth-coloured filling: 1,500 to 2,500 rupees per tooth, depending on size.
- Root canal treatment: 6,500 rupees for a front tooth and 9,000 rupees for a molar.
  The crown that usually follows is charged separately.
- Ceramic crown: 8,000 rupees. Zirconia crown: 14,000 rupees.
- Simple tooth extraction: 1,000 rupees. Wisdom tooth surgical removal: 5,500 rupees.
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic, one session): 9,500 rupees.
- Metal braces: 35,000 rupees for the full course. Clear aligners: from 1,20,000 rupees.
- Dental implant with crown: 38,000 rupees per tooth.
- Digital X-ray: 300 rupees per image. Full-mouth OPG X-ray: 800 rupees.

## Payment and insurance

We accept cash, UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and net banking.
No-cost EMI over 3, 6 or 9 months is available on treatments above 15,000 rupees
with HDFC, ICICI and SBI credit cards.
We are a cashless network provider for Star Health, HDFC Ergo and Niva Bupa.
For other insurers, we provide itemised bills so you can claim a reimbursement.
Cosmetic treatments such as whitening are usually not covered by insurance.

## Appointments

You can book an appointment by phone, on our website, or through this voice assistant.
New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to fill in a medical history form.
Please bring a photo ID, any previous X-rays, and a list of the medicines you take.

### Rescheduling and cancellation
You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment.
Cancellations with less than 4 hours' notice, and missed appointments, are charged
a fee of 300 rupees, which is adjusted against your next visit.
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move you to the next free slot.

## Children's dentistry

We see children from the age of 3. A parent or guardian must stay with children
under 16 during the visit. Our first visit for children includes a check-up,
a fluoride application and a brushing lesson, for 700 rupees.
Children's appointments are available on Saturday mornings at the Banjara Hills branch.

## Emergencies

For severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out tooth or bleeding that will not stop,
call the clinic's main number and press 9 to reach the on-call dentist.
The on-call dentist is available every day from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
Outside those hours, please visit the nearest hospital emergency department.
If a tooth is knocked out, keep it in milk and come in within one hour.

## Hygiene and safety

All instruments are sterilised in a Class B autoclave and sealed until use.
We use single-use needles, gloves and suction tips for every patient.

## Accessibility

The Banjara Hills branch is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible washroom.
The Gachibowli branch is reached by lift. Wheelchairs are available at both branches on request.

## Records and reports

You can ask for copies of your X-rays and treatment notes at any time. They are
emailed within two working days. Records are kept securely for seven years.
